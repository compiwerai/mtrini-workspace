# Code of Conduct

Mtrini Workspace (by Compiwer AI) welcomes everyone building AI for everyone.

- Be respectful, constructive, and inclusive.
- No harassment, hate speech, or unwelcome behavior.
- Critique work, not people; assume good faith.
- Violations may lead to moderation or bans.

Report concerns to the maintainers privately. All contributors agree to these terms by participating.
